Spotifast 0.1.0 is the first stable Pengui release. It brings the library together, keeps side panels the size you chose, and makes the playback controls easier to use.

## New

- **Your library together.** Playlists, saved albums, and podcasts appear in one list by default. Category buttons filter the list; click the selected button again to show everything. The Artists filter is gone. By @pengui07.
- **A side panel that stays where you put it.** Now playing, Lyrics, Queue, and Listening activity share the width you choose. Hide Now playing to leave a slim tab on the right; click anywhere on that tab to restore it. By @pengui07.
- **Hearts in Faithful UI.** Appearance settings can replace song plus/check controls with outline and filled hearts. Shuffle and Repeat also show a dot while enabled. By @pengui07.
- **Playback controls within reach.** Listening activity sits beside Settings, after the visualizer and mini-player buttons. Now playing sits to the right of the volume slider. Listening activity was backported from @dawid-bytys in [crmne/spotifast#909](https://github.com/crmne/spotifast/pull/909); these control changes are by @pengui07.
- **Stable updates from this fork.** The in-app updater checks stable releases from `pengui07/spotifast`. By @pengui07.

## Fixed

- **Click again to dismiss.** Clicking Settings again returns to the previous page, and either Connect button closes its device picker on a second click. By @pengui07.
- **Your queue and Repeat selection survive reopening.** Saved queue rows stay visible while Spotify catches up, and an active playlist's Play button resumes its current song and queue. By @pengui07.
- **Search keeps its room.** The top bar reserves space for its controls, including Listening activity, in narrow windows. By @pengui07.
