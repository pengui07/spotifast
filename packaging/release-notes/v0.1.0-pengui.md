Spotifast 0.1.0-pengui is the first prerelease of the Pengui fork. It brings Faithful visuals, Listening activity, and fixes for keeping your queue and playback controls where you left them.

This is a prerelease with its own version sequence. Install it manually from this page; the app's ordinary update check offers stable releases.

## New

- **Faithful visuals.** An optional Spotify-style layout brings a familiar library, player bar and window controls. Choose the library sort in Settings. By @pengui07.
- **See what people you follow are listening to.** The people button beside Lyrics and Queue opens Listening activity, with links to their songs and playlists. Backported from @dawid-bytys in [crmne/spotifast#909](https://github.com/crmne/spotifast/pull/909).
- **Updates come from this fork.** App update checks and downloads use `pengui07/spotifast` releases. By @pengui07.

## Fixed

- **Your remembered queue stays visible after reopening.** Startup replies cannot clear the saved rows, and saving no longer stops at thirty rows. Duplicate songs keep their places. By @pengui07.
- **Repeat remembers your selection.** Repeat Off, playlist repeat and track repeat survive closing the app and apply when local playback resumes. By @pengui07.
- **A playlist's big Play button resumes its current queue.** Pausing and resuming an active playlist no longer starts a fresh shuffled queue. By @pengui07.
- **Search keeps its space in narrow windows.** Faithful visuals leave room between search and the device controls. By @pengui07.
