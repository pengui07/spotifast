## New

- **Popular releases first.** Artist pages show the five most popular releases. Show more opens the complete discography, sorted newest first, with filters for albums, singles and EPs, and appearances. By @pengui07.
- **Shuffle on artist pages.** Toggle Shuffle beside Play, then start the artist with your selected playback mode. Artist headers also keep their follower count without genre tags. By @pengui07.

## Fixed

- **Albums and playlists rank together.** Playing either moves it above older plays in the combined Library, while pins stay at the top. Playlist custom arrangements and Spotify folders remain available in the Playlists filter. By @pengui07.
- **Complete rows on Home.** The eight shortcuts use two or four columns, avoiding a three-column layout with an incomplete last row. By @pengui07.
